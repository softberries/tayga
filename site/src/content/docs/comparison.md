Tayga is a focused tool: it turns traces and logs into explanations of failing and slow requests. This page will compare it with Jaeger, the Grafana stack (Tempo and Loki), SigNoz, Coroot, OpenObserve and ClickStack/HyperDX, and briefly with Datadog, Dynatrace and New Relic. Every competitor fact will carry a source URL and an access date; what cannot be verified is left out.
