---
title: "Theme and time range"
description: "The theme switch, the time range and live refresh."
---

The theme switch cycles light, dark and system (the default), and the choice is stored in the browser. The time range offers 15m, 1h, 24h and 7d, or a custom past window; Live refreshes every 10 seconds and pauses while the browser tab is hidden.
