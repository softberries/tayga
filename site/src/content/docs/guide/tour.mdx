This page answers **"where do I find what?"** It walks through the parts every page shares, the navigation rail and the header, and says what each page is for, with a link to its full guide.

<Screenshot name="stories-home" eager caption="The Stories page, the home of the app, with the rail on the left and the header at the top." />

## A typical investigation

<Steps>

1. Open **Stories** and pick the top story group: the problem that produced the most failing or slow requests in the range.

2. Read its latest story in the inspector, then select **Open story** to see the root cause, the waterfall, the comparison with normal and the logs.

3. From the story, follow the root-cause service to the **Service map** to see its health and its callers, or a log line to its **log template** to see how often that line appears and whether it alerted.

4. Use **Traces** to find other requests like it, and **Alerts** to see what the logs flagged around the same time.

</Steps>

## The navigation rail

<Screenshot name="shell-rail" caption="The navigation rail." />

The rail on the left links to the five sections. Hover over an icon, or focus it, to see its name. The section you are in is highlighted. On a phone the rail becomes a bar at the bottom of the screen.

The links keep the current [time range](/tayga/guide/time-range/), and leave page filters behind. **Logs and templates** opens the log alerts; the templates are one link away from there.

## The header

<Screenshot name="shell-header" caption="The header, shown on every page." />

- **Page title and breadcrumb** (1): where you are, for example `Logs / Templates / Template 5461…7332`. Long ids are shortened to their first and last four characters.
- **Services degraded**: a red or amber pill, such as `2 services degraded`, appears beside the title when services had errors or ran slow in the last 15 minutes, whatever the page's range. Hover over it to see their names; select it to open the service map over the last 15 minutes. It is hidden while every service is healthy. (Not numbered in the screenshot.)
- **Command palette** (2): "Jump to service, trace id, template…". Select it or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> to jump anywhere. See [Command palette and keyboard shortcuts](/tayga/guide/command-palette/).
- **Time range presets** (3) and **Custom…** (4): the window every page shows. See [Time range and live mode](/tayga/guide/time-range/).
- **Live** (5): refreshes the page every 10 seconds.
- **Theme switch** (6): cycles light, dark and system. See [Theme](/tayga/guide/theme/).
- **User menu**: only when sign-in is turned on. See [Sign-in](/tayga/guide/login/).

Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> once on any page to reveal a **Skip to content** link that jumps past the rail and the header.

## States you will see on every page

- **Loading**: grey placeholders in the shape of the content.
- **A panel that failed**: a red banner, "Could not load …", with the HTTP status, the message and **Try again**. Other panels keep working.
- **A refresh that failed**: the panel keeps its last data and says "Refresh failed · showing data from 14:03:07", with **Try again**.
- **Storage unavailable**: a red banner under the header while the database cannot be reached, with "Retrying on the next refresh." It clears on its own when a request succeeds.
- **Page not found**: an address that matches no page shows "Page not found" with **Go to stories**. The rail and the header stay usable.

## Tips

- Every view's state (filters, selection, open drawer, time range) is in its URL. Copy the address to share exactly what you see.
- Press <kbd>?</kbd> on any page for the list of keyboard shortcuts.
