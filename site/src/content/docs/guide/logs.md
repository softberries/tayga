---
title: "Logs"
description: "Log templates, template detail and the silence toggle."
---

The Logs pages list log templates by count (`/logs/templates`) with search and sparklines, and show a template's detail (`/logs/templates/{id}`): its sample, recent hits, alerts, and the "Silence alert" card that turns silence alerts on for that template.
