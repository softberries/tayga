---
title: "Command palette and shortcuts"
description: "Jump anywhere with Cmd+K or Ctrl+K, and the keyboard shortcuts."
---

`Cmd+K` or `Ctrl+K` opens the command palette: jump to a page, a service, a trace id (32 hex characters), a story group or a template, or switch the theme and the time range. `g` then `s`, `t`, `m`, `l` or `p` goes to Stories, Traces, Service map, Log alerts or Pipeline, and `?` lists the shortcuts.
